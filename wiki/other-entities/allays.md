# Allays

With `haulingMode` set to `allays`, allays become your village's couriers.

- **Only allays you have never given an item to.** Those allays do nothing at all in vanilla, so the mod is giving a job to an idle mob rather than taking one away.  
- **Give an allay an item** and it becomes yours again, behaving exactly as vanilla including note-block delivery.  
- **Take the item back** with an empty hand and it rejoins the hauler pool.

## Hauling and vanilla

Your existing allay farms are untouched, because any allay you have given an item to keeps its vanilla behaviour completely.

Two things change for hauler allays:

- They can take from and put into containers directly  
- **They park at note blocks when idle**, which fixes allays drifting off and getting lost. You never have to play the note block — just place one where you want them to wait.

If your storage fills up, laden allays hover and wait rather than giving up. **A traffic jam of hovering allays is your signal that storage is full.**
