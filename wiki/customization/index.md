Villagers at Work can be tuned at three levels:

- **[Configuration](/customization/configuration/)** — options for server owners and players, in the game's `config` folder: who hauls, how far villagers walk, how animals are bred and culled, and more.
- **[Datapacks](/customization/datapacks/)** — tags and data files for pack authors: which tools, crops, soils, animals, fuels and chests the mod knows, what villagers need on their shelves, and what the wandering trader sells. Most of the mod is data, so a datapack can teach it about another mod without touching a jar.
- **[Extension API](/customization/extension-api/)** — for mod developers: bring your own guards to the armories, speak for your villagers, and more.
