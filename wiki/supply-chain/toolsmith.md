# Toolsmith

Makes tools for other villagers, on demand. It watches nearby workstations, and when a station's shelf is missing its tool, it forges one and delivers it.

It makes hoes for farmers, culling axes for butchers, and Farmer's Delight knives for both. With `toolsmithMakesHoes` off it makes no hoes, and a farmer gets one only from you. Shears and fishing rods are not its job — shepherds and fishermen make their own.

**Stock better material and you get better tools.** It uses the best available, working down diamond → iron → copper→ stone →gold → wood planks.

**Where two tools are the same metal, the station's need decides.** A butcher's culling tool is an axe or a Farmer's Delight knife, and the knife is what makes the better drops, so with Farmer's Delight installed the knife is what it forges. Material still comes first: stock diamond and you get a diamond axe rather than an iron knife.
