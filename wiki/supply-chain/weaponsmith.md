# Weaponsmith

Makes swords for MCA swordsman guards, one at a time, placing into its armory until a guard’s weapon breaks and takes one

It needs an armory, which is the only place what it makes can go, and shows a plain chest until it has one. It works without a guard mod too: with no guards to take from it, it makes its sword once and keeps the armory stocked for show.

Its armory can be an **armor stand** instead of a chest, and one stand between the grindstone and an armorer's blast furnace serves both: the armorer dresses it and the weaponsmith puts the sword in its hand, a swordsman's whole kit in one place.

**Tools:** None 

**Consumables:**

| Sticks | Mandatory |
| :---- | :---- |
| Ingots, stones or planks | Mandatory to have any of them |

**Suppliers:**

| Player | Planks |
| :---- | :---- |
| Armorer | Ingots |
| Fisherman | Sticks |

**Customers:** MCA Swordsman guards
