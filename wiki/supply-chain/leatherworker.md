sidebar:
  order: 8
---

Makes leather armour at its cauldron, which is the archer's tier of gear. It makes one of each armor piece and puts them into their armory until a guard breaks their armor and needs replacement.

It needs an armory, which is the only place what it makes can go, and shows a plain chest until it has one. It works without a guard mod too: with no guards to take from it, it makes its set of leather armour once and keeps the armory stocked for show.

**Stock a dye** and the armour comes out coloured — the cauldron's real vanilla function, and a nice way to give your archers a uniform.

**Tools:** None
