Makes arrows or bows for its armory, where MCA archer guards take them — but not both at once. Three shelf slots will not hold the ingredients for both, so a village that wants both builds two fletching tables.

It needs an armory, which is the only place what it makes can go, and shows a plain chest until it has one. It works without a guard mod too: with no guards to take from it, it makes its arrows or bow once and keeps the armory stocked for show.

A fletcher making bows can use an **armor stand** as its armory instead of a chest, and one stand between the fletching table and a leatherworker's cauldron serves both: leather armour on the stand and the bow in its hand. Arrows always go in a chest.

| Making | Stock |
| :---- | :---- |
| Arrows | Flint \+ feathers \+ sticks |
| Bows | Sticks \+ strings |

**Tools:** None 

**Consumables:**

| Flint | Mandatory for Arrow maker |
| :---- | :---- |
| Feathers | Mandatory for Arrow maker |
| Sticks | Mandatory |
| Strings | Mandatory for Bow maker |

**Suppliers:**

| :---- | :---- |

| Shepherd | Strings |
| [Lumberjack](/more-villagers/lumberjack/) | Sticks |

**Customers:**
