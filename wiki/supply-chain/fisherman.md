Fishes from nearby rivers or oceans, casting its line as you cast yours: with the mod's client part installed you see the line and the bobber, and the bobber goes under when a fish bites. Supplies half the village from the junk it catches. Fish, junk and treasures which have no customers go to storage. Rotten flesh is the only scarce input in the enchanting loop, and fishing is the only automated source of it. 

**Half of what it catches is junk**, against about one catch in ten for a player, because the junk — string, sticks, leather, bones, bowls — is what the village needs from it. `fishingJunkChance` sets how much; your own fishing is unchanged.

**With Villagers after Work installed**, after work it cooks fish for the village at the campfires near it, from the catch it kept back (see [Villagers after Work](/after-work/)).

**With [More Villagers at Work](/more-villagers/) installed**, it brings a Chef's kitchen the fish its meals are made from, and the bowls and water bottles it catches, up to a stack of each, once its customers' shelves are seen to and before storage. **Half of the fish it catches that the Chef uses go straight to the Chef**: each one has an even chance, as it comes out of the water, of being taken to the Chef's kitchen there and then, even a single fish. The rest go as usual: with Villagers after Work it keeps its dinner from them first, and then each kind goes to the Chef until its kitchen holds a stack of it, as with every ingredient, and only then to storage. It takes the Chef what it has as soon as it has it, however little. Fish the Chef's meals don't use, such as pufferfish and tropical fish, never go to it, and with no Chef that uses fish nothing changes.

**It fills empty bottles with water for the clerics.** A villager that drinks one of the [healing potions](/general-mechanics/healing/) brings the empty bottle straight to its shelf, up to 16 there; the shelf never asks for bottles, so you need not put one on it, and the fisherman uses every one. While a cleric's brewing stand has room for water bottles, it fills the bottles from its shelf at the water it fishes and puts them straight into that stand, and only a cleric's. A stand has room while it is empty or holds water bottles or healing or regeneration potions. **The water bottles it catches** go to a cleric's stand or, with More Villagers at Work, a Chef's kitchen, either one at random, and to storage only when neither wants one. A Chef's kitchen holding eight bottles, whatever is in them, wants no more.

It makes its own fishing rods, so it needs nothing from anyone. A rod is all it needs to fish; sticks and string are only what it makes the next one from, and it keeps itself stocked with them out of its own catch. **It keeps eight sticks on its shelf**, enough for two rods with some over, and the sticks it catches beyond those go to its customers.
