---
title: Success and Error indicators
---

The mod tells you what it is doing without you ever opening a menu.

## When something works

A villager emits **the green star particles** when a shelf binds to its workstation or when a chest binds. That is your confirmation that placement worked. No particle means it did not bind.

## When something is wrong

A blocked villager tries to show you **the missing mandatory item it needs as an icon above its head**, which persists so you can spot the stuck station from across the village. If the problem is not specific to a single item, it shows the vanilla angry particles.

| What it shows | What it means |
| :---- | :---- |
| A tool or material | It cannot work without this — stock the shelf |
| Copper chest | Permanent storage is full or unreachable |
| Shelf | Shelf binding is ambiguous or shared — too many shelves, or one shared between stations |
| Angry particles | More than one mandatory item is missing. |
| Armor stand | More than one armor stand, or the stand is shared with another maker of the same kind: two armour makers, or two weapon makers |
| Plain chest | Armory is missing, for a weaponsmith, fletcher or leatherworker, or its only chest also touches another maker of the same kind: two armour makers, or two weapon makers. Shown once the shelf has what the villager needs. Never shown by the armorer, whose armory is optional |
| Fish | No nearby water for Fisherman to fish |

**With MCA Reborn extension installed**, all of this is also spoken aloud and written to chat in plain language, which is considerably easier than reading icons.

**Errors show only during working hours.**

## What is *not* an error

Any missing optional item.

One thing that looks like it is there still counts as missing: **the last of an item a shelf keeps as its marker.** A butcher's last wheat, a fletcher's last flint and a cleric's last nether wart stay on the shelf to say what it is for, and are never used, so a shelf down to one has nothing left to work with. Once the villager lacks nothing else, it shows that item.
