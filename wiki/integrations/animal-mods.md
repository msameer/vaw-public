**Alex's Mobs** and similar are supported through tags rather than mod-specific code, so either Fabric continuation works identically.

**Alex's Mobs farm animals work out of the box:**

| Animal | Fed on | Butcher | Shepherd |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Bison | Wheat | Culls it for beef and bison fur | Shears it for bison fur |
| Emu | Wheat | Culls it for feathers, and picks up the eggs it lays | – |
| Gazelle | Wheat, acacia blossom | Culls it for mutton and gazelle horns | – |

- **One herd, two workers** — the butcher culls and breeds the bison, and the shepherd shears whichever have fur, leaving alone the one the butcher is bringing in.
- **Shearable animals** — a shepherd shears a mod's animal that works with vanilla shears, once it is a farm animal or in the shearable tag. Nothing else modded is sheared until you opt it in.
- **Farm animals** ship as a conservative default list: cow, pig, sheep, goat, rabbit, chicken, and Alex's Mobs' bison, emu and gazelle.

>   
> **Be careful adding modded animals to the farm-animal tag.** Many modded creatures fight back — an Alex's Mobs grizzly bear will kill your butcher. Extending the tag is at your own risk.
