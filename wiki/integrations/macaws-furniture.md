Supported out of the box: villagers sit on its seats and store in its cupboards.

**Seats**, with [Villagers after Work](/after-work/):

- **Chairs, stools, couches and chaises** are seats, at a table or around a campfire, like any other.
- **At a Macaw's table**, chairs pushed against it or two blocks out with a block of room between, as for any high table.

**Storage:**

- **Drawers, wardrobes and kitchen cabinets** are village storage, as copper chests are.
- **Touching a workstation, one is that station's drop-off**, as a plain chest is: the worker puts its goods there and haulers take them to storage.
- **Counters with drawers or a cupboard**, and the bookshelf drawers and cupboards, are storage too.
- **Plain counters, desks, sinks and bookshelves** are not.
- Villagers put their goods in any of them within reach, so keep your own things elsewhere.

**Macaw's other mods** need nothing:

- **Fences and Walls**: leashed animals pass through its gates, as through vanilla ones.
- **Doors**: villagers open its wooden doors.
- **Roofs**: a bed under one counts as under a roof for bedtime breeding.
- **Windows**, with Villagers after Work: villagers close the shutters, blinds and windows near their beds at night and open them in the morning, blinds all the way up. Its curtains, and windows locked with its key, are left alone.
- **Lights**, with Villagers after Work: any of its lights with an on and off, such as lamps, lava lamps, street lamps and candle holders, near a campfire or a seat are lit after work and put out at bedtime, as candles are. One beside a villager's bed is lit as it goes to bed.
