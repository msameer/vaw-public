Addons for [Farmer's Delight](/integrations/farmers-delight/) whose meals stand on a table. The farmer grows their crops and the [Chef](/more-villagers/chef/) cooks their meals.

**Rustic Delight**

- **Farmer** — grows bell peppers of every colour, cotton and coffee, replanting from the harvest as it does wheat
- **Chef** — cooks its pancakes, cheesecakes and feasts, and makes the batter and syrup they take in the pot, and the stuffed bell peppers its medleys take, each colour as its peppers come in. Villagers eat pancakes off the stack one at a time

**Ube's Delight**

- **Farmer** — grows ube, garlic and ginger as it does potatoes. It cuts lemongrass leaves and leaves the stalk to grow more, and sows lemongrass seeds beside the lemongrass it grows
- **Chef** — cooks its cakes, feasts and leaf feasts. An empty leaf feast is no meal: it lays one only with food on it

**Ocean's Delight: Expanded**

- **Chef** — cooks its guardian soup from a guardian you bring, and with [Display Delight](/more-villagers/chef/#with-display-delight) its other dishes too

**Millet Delight**

- **Farmer** — grows millet from its panicles, as it does wheat
- **Chef** — bakes millet cakes from millet you bring
- **Wild millet** drops a panicle too, so you can start a field. With Villagers at Work installed, millet and its bags and bales drop what they should, which this version of the mod forgets
