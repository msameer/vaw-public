# Farmer's Delight

Supported throughout, touching three professions.

**Farmer**

- **Rice** — harvested from the bank, taking only the panicles so the plant regrows. Rice it carries is planted from the bank too, into open water beside rice already growing, so a paddy fills out and the pond that keeps your farmland moist is left alone  
- **Tomatoes** — harvested by hand so the vine survives and keeps producing, including vines climbing ropes up to three blocks. A vine that is lost from a tomato row is replanted from the farmer's own tomatoes  
- **Cabbage and onion** — handled like any other crop  
- **Mushrooms** — brown and red mushroom colonies on Rich Soil. The farmer cuts a full colony with its knife for three mushrooms, and the colony grows again; without a knife it breaks the colony for five and plants one back. With a hoe it plants the mushrooms it carries on bare Rich Soil beside its colonies, and on no other ground. A field of mushrooms alone is never tilled. Its bone meal goes on colonies as on its other crops  
- **Rich Soil** — recognised, preferred over plain dirt, and tilled into Rich Soil Farmland. The farmer does not *make* Rich Soil; you supply it.  
- **Knife** — harvesting rice with a knife gives rice and straw directly instead of panicles that need a cutting board

**Butcher** — culls with an FD knife, never an axe. The knife guarantees an animal's secondary drop (a cow gives 1–3 leather instead of 0–2) and pigs can drop **Ham**, which is obtainable no other way. A butcher still holding an axe puts it in storage and takes the knife from its shelf.

**Toolsmith** — makes knives for farmers and butchers, and no axes for butchers.

**The Chef** — with [More Villagers at Work]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link more-villagers.md %}), a profession of its own that works at a Farmer's Delight cabinet and cooks its feasts and pies on its stoves, from what the farmer, the butcher and the fisherman bring. See [Chef]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link more-villagers/chef.md %}).
