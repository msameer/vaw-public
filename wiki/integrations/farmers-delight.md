# Farmer's Delight

Supported throughout, touching three professions.

**Farmer**

- **Rice** — harvested from the bank, taking only the panicles so the plant regrows. Rice it carries is planted from the bank too, into open water beside rice already growing, so a paddy fills out and the pond that keeps your farmland moist is left alone  
- **Tomatoes** — harvested by hand so the vine survives and keeps producing, including vines climbing ropes up to three blocks. A vine that is lost from a tomato row is replanted from the farmer's own tomatoes  
- **Cabbage and onion** — handled like any other crop  
- **Rich Soil** — recognised, preferred over plain dirt, and tilled into Rich Soil Farmland. The farmer does not *make* Rich Soil; you supply it.  
- **Knife** — harvesting rice with a knife gives rice and straw directly instead of panicles that need a cutting board

**Butcher** — an FD knife is better than an axe for culling. It guarantees an animal's secondary drop (a cow gives 1–3 leather instead of 0–2) and pigs can drop **Ham**, which is obtainable no other way.

**Toolsmith** — makes knives.
