# Shelves

The shelf is the heart of the mod.

- Place a **shelf** touching a workstation — any of the six faces, including above and below.  
- The villager that claims the workstation is now working.  
- A shelf has **three slots**. It’s where the villager gets the tools to use and the items to consume for production.

**One shelf per workstation, one workstation per shelf.** 

**Three slots is deliberately tight.** It is what pushes you to build more workstations and employ more villagers rather than running everything through one overworked smith.
