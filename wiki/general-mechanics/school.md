A librarian holds school for the village. It is for the look of it: nothing is learned.

## Setting it up

- **A lectern with a book and quill on it**, worked by a librarian with **no shelf** touching the lectern. A librarian with a shelf makes enchanted books instead, and holds no school.
- **Seats within 6 blocks of the lectern, on the same level**: carpets, cushions, or another mod's chairs (`vaw:seats`, `vaw:seat_blocks`; see [Datapacks](/customization/datapacks/)).

## School hours

- **School is held in the first third of the working day.**
- **Children, unemployed villagers and nitwits** within 32 blocks walk to a free seat and sit facing the lectern until school ends. They take one seat each, and one that finds no free seat carries on with its day.
- **Children run their errands after school.**
- **The teacher stands behind its lectern** during school hours, on one of the three blocks on the side its book faces, facing the class. Put the seats on the other side.
- **School waits** for the night, a panic or a raid, as work does.

## The teacher's books

- **A teacher sells short books about the mod**: one on each of its general mechanics, and one on each profession. A librarian that holds no school sells none.
- **It sells more as it levels up**: the first general books as a novice, the rest as an apprentice, then the professions.
- **Each costs as many emeralds as its level**: one at novice, up to five at master.
- **Books on the Chef, the beekeeper and the lumberjack** are sold only with [More Villagers at Work](/more-villagers/) installed, the Chef's only with Farmer's Delight too.
- **Players without the mod read them in English**; with it, in their own language.
- **Own them all** for the *Required Reading* advancement.

## With MCA Reborn

- **Children and teens go to school.** Babies and toddlers stay away.
- **Unemployed MCA villagers go too.**
- **A villager you have told to stay, to follow you, or to do a chore** is left alone.
