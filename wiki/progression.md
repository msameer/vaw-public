# Progression

The mod has a natural arc that follows your world's own progression.

**Tier 1 — before copper.** Villagers work and fill their local containers. You empty them by hand. Fully functional, entirely manual.

**Tier 2 — the copper age.** You have copper chests and possibly golems. Villagers walk their own output to storage and golems sort it. It works, but every profession loses shift time to the walk.

**Tier 3 — haulers.** Something else does the walking and villagers stay at their stations. Under `allays` this is a real reward: you have to find allays in pillager outposts or woodland mansions and duplicate them with amethyst. Under `villagers` this is basically merged with **Tier 2**.

Each tier **removes a cost** rather than adding a mechanic, so there is nothing new to learn at any step.

In a single-player world, the mod's own advancements tab marks the same arc as it happens: see [Advancements]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link progression/advancements.md %}).
