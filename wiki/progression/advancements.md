Villagers at Work has its own advancements tab. It follows your village as it grows: the first ones teach you how the mod works, and the rest mark each supply chain as it starts running.

## Who earns them

**Only the owner of a single-player world.** On a server there is no tab at all, and if you open your world to LAN, your guests earn nothing.

Villagers do the work, not you, so you earn an advancement the first time the moment happens anywhere in your world, wherever you are standing.

## Who did it

Earning one is announced in chat, as vanilla's are. **When a villager involved has a name, a line saying what it did comes first**, and when two villagers are involved both are named:

> Ruth the cartographer took paper to Oswald the librarian.

A villager without a name is called by its job: *Ruth the cartographer took paper to a librarian.* With MCA Reborn every villager has a name. A vanilla villager has one once you give it a name tag.

Both lines follow the `announceAdvancements` gamerule.

## The advancements

| Branch | Advancement | You earn it when |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Start | **Villagers at Work** | A shelf binds to a villager's workstation |
| Start | **Read the Signs** | A villager shows you what it needs |
| Start | **Problem Solved** | A blocked villager gets back to work |
| Start | **Supply Chain** | One villager supplies another |
| Start | **Out of the Way** | A villager puts its surplus in a copper chest |
| Enchanting | **Paper Trail** | A cartographer's paper reaches a librarian |
| Enchanting | **Rotten Luck** | A cleric turns rotten flesh into lapis for a librarian |
| Enchanting | **Well Read** | A librarian delivers an enchanted book |
| Enchanting | **Spare Parts** | A toolsmith delivers a tool |
| Enchanting | **Enchanted Workshop** | A toolsmith delivers an enchanted tool |
| Farming | **Field Hand** | A farmer brings in a harvest |
| Farming | **Gone Fishing** | A fisherman lands a catch |
| Farming | **Herd Instinct** | A butcher breeds a pair |
| Farming | **Shear Pleasure** | A shepherd shears a sheep |
| Military | **Iron Age** | An armorer smelts ingots for another villager |
| Military | **Sharpened** | A weaponsmith stocks its armory with a sword |
| Military | **Fletched** | A fletcher stocks its armory with a bow or arrows |
| Military | **Uniform** | A leatherworker makes dyed armour |
| Military | **Shield Wall** | An armorer makes a shield |
| Military | **On Display** | A maker dresses its armor stand |
| Military | **Fully Stocked** | A maker fills its armory |
| Military | **Standing Army** | The armorer's, weaponsmith's, fletcher's and leatherworker's armories are all full at once |
| Logistics | **Heavy Lifting** | A hauler carries a load to storage |
| Logistics | **Trade Route** | A villager restocks its shelf from the wandering trader |
| Healing | **Brewing Up** | A cleric starts a batch of potions |
| Healing | **Gilded** | A farmer makes a glistering melon slice for a cleric |
| Healing | **Healthy Village** | A hurt villager drinks a potion from a cleric's brewing stand |
| | **All Hands** | All twelve professions are at work at once |
| | **Required Reading** | You own every book a village teacher sells (see [School](/general-mechanics/school/)) |

**With the MCA Reborn extension**, four more join the same tab:

| Branch | Advancement | You earn it when |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Military | **On Guard** | A guard takes gear from an armory |
| Military | **Suited Up** | A guard wears armour in all four slots from the village's armories |
| Military | **Spoils** | A guard brings its loot to a cleric |
| Healing | **Patched Up** | A hurt guard drinks a potion before it gears up |

A shelf that was already beside a workstation when you installed the mod does not earn *Villagers at Work* by itself: place a new one, or wait for a villager to take a job at one.
